About

I'm a civil engineer specializing in transportation planning and intelligent transportation systems. My work combines traffic simulation, travel demand modeling and reinforcement learning, using tools like SUMO, VISSIM and PTV Visum to model how networks behave, and MARL to explore how signals can coordinate rather than act alone.

Before starting my Master's, I worked as a traffic analyst and, briefly, as a structural engineer, which keeps my research grounded in how transportation systems are actually built and studied in practice.

Research

Multi-agent RL for corridor signal coordination — MSc thesis, in progress

Designing a cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning approach to coordinate traffic signals along a two-intersection corridor in SUMO, using an OSM-derived network. The study compares independent and cooperative PPO controllers against fixed-time and classical green-wave baselines. Supervisor: Prof. Mahmoud Saffarzadeh.

SUMO TraCI PettingZoo Stable-Baselines3 PPO

Tehran demand and traffic assignment models — SUMO

Developing SUMO-based demand and traffic assignment models for Tehran land uses (hospitals, university campus, recreational center), using the Tehran Trip Generation Manual and a TAZ-level OD matrix. Python scripts generate the network and zone reports. In a related team project on reducing through-traffic in residential neighborhoods, I built the OSM-based network model and estimated demand from the Tehran Comprehensive Plan.

Kerman Municipality traffic study, Report 1: Shora Square — team project, 2026

A team study by Tarbiat Modares University covering macroscopic modeling in Visum, microsimulation of road-closure scenarios, and multi-criteria evaluation of the options. My role: report writing and analysis of traffic scenarios.

Transportation demand modeling — PTV Visum

Built and calibrated a 5-zone network model for Tehran, including OD matrix calibration via the gravity model (KALIBRI), logit-based modal split, and PrT/PuT assignment. Full report and code published on GitHub.

Deep RL for adaptive signal control — seminar project

Reviewed the literature on deep reinforcement learning (PPO) for adaptive traffic signal control, and developed a reinforcement learning controller for a single intersection in SUMO. This work is the starting point for the thesis.
