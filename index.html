About

I'm a civil engineer specializing in transportation planning and intelligent transportation systems. My work sits at the intersection of traffic simulation and reinforcement learning — using tools like SUMO, VISSIM, and PTV Visum to model how networks behave, and MARL to explore how signals can coordinate rather than act alone.

Before starting my Master's, I worked as a traffic analyst and, briefly, as a structural engineer — experience that keeps my research grounded in how transportation systems are actually built and studied in practice.

Research

Multi-agent RL for corridor signal coordination — MSc thesis, in progress

Developing a cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning approach to coordinate traffic signals across a two-intersection corridor, using SUMO with an OSM-derived network. Builds on an earlier single-intersection PPO controller to study how coordination changes emergent signal behavior.
